Who's who at Nan's kitchen table

Nan works every pick out herself, pencil behind the ear and a cuppa going cold. Horses: she watches the bookies like a hawk and nudges their market by recent form. Dogs and harness: each runner's record, its last few runs and where it jumps from.

Pop is FormFav's prediction model. He bought a computer in 1998 and has trusted it ever since.

Bob is Krok's KrokBot model. Nobody's quite sure what Bob does, but he's very confident about it.

Gran backs the bookies' favourite. Hasn't read a form guide since the Melbourne Cup was on black and white telly, and isn't about to start.

The Panel averages the lot: a family argument that ends in a group hug.

Where Nan gets her gossip

Horse prices and results come from PuntersEdge; form from FormFav. Dog and harness prices are Nan's own guesses. When FormFav's form runs dry, she borrows quick form from PuntersEdge (the market plus recent runs). If PuntersEdge is having a lie-down too, Krok's cards fill in, marked Price only: no form there, so Nan goes on the market alone, muttering.

Nan's record keeps her honest. Her top pick in every horse race, and every race at the dog and harness meetings she follows, is written down before the jump and checked against the official results. Profit is $1 on the nose of every top pick at the official win dividend. Scratched picks don't count, and no, she can't rub them out afterwards.
