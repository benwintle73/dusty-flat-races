How to play

Tap a square. It and the squares touching it (not diagonals) move one colour along: dark → cyan → magenta → dark.

Make every square match its target. The little dot in each square's corner shows its target when they differ.

Match par for ★★★. Hints are free but cost the third star. Striped squares are blocked: they never change and don't pass pulses on.
