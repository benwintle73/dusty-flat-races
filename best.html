HOW BEST BETS ARE PICKED

Nan's Tips looks at every race about 90 minutes before it jumps, using all the tipsters and The Mob (everyone's chances blended). A bet only goes in when one really stands out, so some days there are a few and some days plenty. One bet a race, and once it's in it's locked, the same for everyone.

Win: The Mob's top pick at 40% or better, most of the tipsters agree, at $4.50 or shorter.
Each way: a strong chance (28%+) at $4 to $15, or a value runner at $4 to $8.
Roughie: $8 or longer, where the form models rate it well above its price.
Trifecta: seven or more runners and the top three well clear of the rest, boxed.
